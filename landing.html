Game Features

🎮

3D Exploration

Explore immersive 3D environments rendered with cutting-edge WebGL technology. Discover hidden creatures and secrets across the Great Forest.

⚡

Creature Collection

Discover and collect unique Vectormons with distinct abilities and characteristics. Build your personal collection and complete the Vectormon Pokedex.

🌐

Browser-Based

Play directly in your browser with no downloads required. Fully compatible with modern browsers supporting WebGL and JavaScript.

💾

Local Progress Saving

Your game progress is automatically saved locally in your browser. Resume your adventure anytime from where you left off.

Mobile Friendly

Optimized for both desktop and mobile devices. Experience smooth gameplay across all screen sizes and resolutions.

Interactive Gameplay

Engage in interactive battles, complete quests, and unlock achievements as you progress through the game.

How to Play

  • Explore: Navigate through the Great Forest using intuitive 3D controls
  • Discover: Find and encounter unique Vectormon creatures in the wild
  • Capture: Use Capture Spheres to add Vectormons to your collection
  • Train: Level up your creatures and enhance their abilities
  • Battle: Challenge other trainers and test your team's strength
  • Collect: Complete your collection and unlock special rewards
